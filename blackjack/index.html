How the trainer works
  • Every decision is checked against basic strategy for these house rules (6 decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double after split, late surrender).
  • Each optimal decision adds 1 to your score. A mistake resets your score to 0.
  • Show best move opens a basic strategy chart with your hand highlighted, and marks the right button. It resets your score to 0, and that decision isn't scored.
  • If the best play isn't available (for example, you can't afford to double), the next-best play counts as correct.
  • Basic strategy always declines insurance and even money.
  • Accuracy counts every scored decision. Your best score and accuracy are saved in this browser.
House rules
  • Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards count 10, aces count 1 or 11.
  • A two-card 21 (Blackjack) pays 3:2. Other wins pay 1:1. Ties push and your bet is returned.
  • The dealer draws to 16 and stands on all 17s, including soft 17.
  • If the dealer shows an ace or a ten, they check for Blackjack before you act. If they have it, the hand ends right away.
  • Insurance: offered when the dealer shows an ace. It costs half your bet and pays 2:1 if the dealer has Blackjack. If you hold Blackjack, it's offered as even money, a guaranteed 1:1 payout.
  • Double: on any first two cards (including after a split). Your bet doubles and you get exactly one more card.
  • Split: any two cards of equal value, up to 4 hands. Split aces get one card each and can't be re-split. A 21 after a split pays 1:1, not 3:2.
  • Surrender: on your first two cards (not after a split), give up the hand and get half your bet back.
  • The shoe is reshuffled once fewer than 25% of the cards remain.
